## 2. What learners say is missing

1. **Theory ≠ skill.** Recurring Reforge critique: "too theoretical, doesn't teach real skills."
2. **Overpriced relative to substance.** $4,000+ certs "could be found elsewhere for less"; Reforge renewals negotiated down ~50%.
3. **Certs don't get you hired; demonstrated thinking does.** Hiring managers want candidates who can "define problems, propose solutions, justify decisions" via portfolios; recruiters are leery of cert-stackers ([BrainStation](https://brainstation.io/career-guides/is-a-product-management-certificate-worth-it), [aakashg.com](https://www.aakashg.com/best-certifications-for-product-managers/)).
4. **No feedback loop.** The only graded PM reps today: $100–250/hr coaches, uneven peer mocks, or the job itself — the chicken-and-egg for aspiring PMs.
5. **Nothing is mobile.** Every practice-first product is desktop web.

## 3. Job market

- PM openings at a 2-year high, +53.6% from the 2023 trough ([Lenny's tracker](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/state-of-the-product-job-market-in)); India +42% YoY.
- **AI PM is the hottest wedge** (688 open AI PM roles tracked; $2,750–$5k AI PM certs selling briskly).
- ~850k active PMs globally, 2.6M+ LinkedIn titles; Exponent claims 1M+ candidates. Career-switcher prep budgets commonly **$500–$3,000**.
- No certification is a gatekeeper. 77% of hiring managers call certs "a differentiator, not a requirement" — a skill-verification score could out-signal a certificate.

## 4. Monetization analogies (learn-by-doing winners)

| Company | Model | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| **Duolingo** | Super ~$12.99/mo; **Max $29.99/mo** (AI Video Call/Roleplay is the premium justifier) | $1.04B revenue, 52.7M DAU, 12.2M paying ([Q4 FY25](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828025049514/q3fy25duolingo9-30x25share.htm)) |
| **Brilliant** | ~$161.88/yr, practice-only, no video lectures | Proof "reps not videos" sells at ~$13.50/mo |
| **Chess.com** | Tiers to $119/yr; Elo as identity | ~$150M revenue, ~2M paying ([Sherwood](https://sherwood.news/culture/how-the-chess-com-empire-makes-more-than-usd100m-a-year/)) |
| **DataCamp** | ~$25/mo; Teams $27/seat | Practice platform → team-plan expansion template |

**Transferable playbook:** the practice loop is the free hook; **feedback quality is the paywall** (Duolingo Max and chess.com Game Review both monetize AI feedback). A visible skill rating doubles as retention engine and hiring signal. Consumer price gravity: **$10–15/mo annualized, $120–170/yr**, with a proven $25–30/mo AI-premium tier.

## 5. AI-native entrants (2025+)

- **Sandbox4PM** — watch closely; beat it on mobile, game feel, and artifact breadth.
- **Exponent AI mocks** — rubric-graded, paid-only, beta.
- **Practica** — AI career coach $10–20/mo; chat advice, not graded practice.
- **ChatPRD** — PMs pay AI to *write* PRDs; nobody has built the AI that teaches you to write them *well* and proves it.

## 6. Market-gap synthesis

Plot passive→practice against desktop/cohort→mobile/daily-habit:
- Passive + expensive: Reforge, Product School, Maven.
- Practice + desktop: GoPractice, ProductDo, Sandbox4PM, Exponent.
- **Practice + mobile + gamified: effectively vacant.**

**Six wedges nobody covers together:** (1) AI-graded artifacts (the documents PMs are judged on); (2) stakeholder roleplay at consumer price; (3) spaced repetition of judgment; (4) a skill rating that replaces certificates; (5) career-long use, not interview-only; (6) iOS-native habit product.

**Viable pricing:** Free (daily drill, streak, limited grades) · Core **$11.99–14.99/mo, ~$79–129/yr** · Premium AI **$24.99–29.99/mo** · Certification/rating **$99–299 one-time** · Teams **$25–30/seat** later.
