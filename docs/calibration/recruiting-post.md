## r/ProductManagement post

**Title:** I'm testing whether AI can grade PM work like a senior PM does — paying experienced PMs to prove (or disprove) it

**Body:**

I'm building a practice tool for PMs — write real deliverables (one-page PRDs, experiment plans, strategy memos), get scored against a visible rubric. The obvious question is whether AI grading is any good, and I don't want to answer it with vibes.

So I'm running a blind calibration study: 30 anonymized trainee-level submissions, each scored independently by a panel of senior PMs and by the grader. Then I publish the agreement numbers, whatever they say. If the grader can't sit inside the panel's own agreement range, that's the ballgame and I'll say so publicly.

**What I need:** 3–5 PMs with 5+ years who have actually reviewed other people's work (hiring loops, design reviews, managing PMs). ~3–4 hours of async scoring against defined rubrics, on your own schedule, paid. You'd be credited in the published methodology if you want to be.

**What you get besides payment:** the full results, and the odd experience of finding out where you and other senior PMs disagree about what "good" looks like (in pilot runs, that part surprised people more than the AI did).

Comment or DM if interested. Happy to share the rubrics up front — they're part of the product, not a secret.

## Screening questions (use in DMs)

1. Years in product roles, and current/most recent title?
2. Have you reviewed other PMs' written work in a formal setting (hiring, calibration, design review)? Roughly how often?
3. Any industry you know cold? (We spread items across SaaS/fintech/marketplace/consumer/healthcare — coverage helps.)
4. Can you complete scoring within 10 days of receiving the pack?

Prefer raters from different companies/backgrounds — panel diversity is what makes the ceiling honest.
