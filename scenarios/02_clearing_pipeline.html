Scenario 02

The Clearing Pipeline — Moomoo

Role: Senior Product Manager — Brokerage Clearing and Settlement System, Moomoo Canada
Domain: Internal platform · post-trade infrastructure · operational tools
Stakeholders: Brokerage Ops · Risk · Compliance · Treasury · Engineering
Vendors: DTCC · OCC · CDS · CDCC · Broadridge
Timeframe: 6 iterations (6-week quarter)
Difficulty: Advanced (matches the JD)
Target skills: Platform PM · vendor integration · regulated operations · incident response · post-trade trade lifecycle

Interview relevance: This scenario is built specifically for the Moomoo JD "Senior Product Manager — Brokerage Clearing and Settlement System." It leverages Mike's Questrade ops/risk/treasury experience and rehearses the exact tensions this role will face.

1. Setup & Premise

You've been hired as Senior PM for Moomoo Canada's Clearing and Settlement platform — the internal post-trade system that handles everything that happens after a customer hits "Buy": trade capture, clearing (DTCC/OCC/CDS), netting, settlement (T+1 DVP), reconciliation, corporate actions, regulatory reporting.

Your "customers" are internal: Brokerage Operations, Risk, Treasury, Compliance, and the customer-facing product team who depends on your platform not breaking. Your "users" are Ops analysts who spend all day in dashboards you own.

Moomoo Canada shipped 6 months ago on a mostly-imported platform from HQ (Hong Kong). The platform works for HK's CCASS workflow but was never built for North American clearing. The team has been patching — T+1 settlement workarounds, manual OCC exercise/assignment processing, Broadridge reconciliation in Excel. You're here to turn the patches into a real product.

Your quarter-ending ask from leadership: "Get us off operational heroics. Reduce manual break resolution by 50%. Pass the CIRO systems audit in Q3. Don't break anything."

What's true at iteration 1

  • Daily trade volume: ~120,000 trades/day across equity + options + FX
  • Break rate: ~0.8% of trades break → ~960 breaks/day requiring manual analyst intervention
  • Ops team: 14 analysts spending ~60% of their time on reconciliation
  • US options clearing (OCC): exercise/assignment processing is 100% manual on expiry days — 500+ accounts/monthly cycle
  • Corporate actions: handled by a third party with 48-hour latency; customer complaints about dividend timing
  • Broadridge integration: nightly batch file via SFTP; when it fails, manual re-run required
  • CIRO Systems Audit: scheduled for Q3; last audit flagged 12 deficiencies around trade surveillance
  • T+1 settlement: Canada moved to T+1 in May 2024 alongside US; Moomoo's platform is handling it but with 3× the ops workload
  • HK HQ platform team: responsible for core trading engine; slow to prioritize Canada-specific changes
  • Engineering team: 4 devs + 1 QA. Two senior, two juniors. One just gave notice.

Platform constraints (structural)

  • HQ controls trade-capture engine; you own post-trade flows downstream
  • CIRO systems requirements are non-negotiable — platform must meet Rule 2600 standards
  • Regulatory reporting deadlines are hard (CIRO, OSC, FINTRAC); missing them means fines
  • DTCC/OCC/CDS have rigid connectivity protocols; vendor change is multi-month
  • T+1 settlement means no second chances; fails escalate to DTCC penalties rapidly
